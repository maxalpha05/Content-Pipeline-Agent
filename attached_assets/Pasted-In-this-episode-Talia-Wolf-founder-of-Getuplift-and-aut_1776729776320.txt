In this episode, Talia Wolf, founder of Getuplift and author of Emotional Targeting, dismantles the idea that AI can handle the emotional layer of your marketing.

She explains why the explosion of AI-generated copy has made B2B websites functionally identical, why scraping one Reddit thread and feeding it to ChatGPT gives you surface-level insights that miss the actual psychology driving purchases, and why "trust matters" as a takeaway is useless unless you understand that trust means letting a stranger into your house and hoping they don't break something.

We pressure-tested the entire emotional targeting framework against a world where buyers are using LLMs to pre-filter decisions before they ever hit your website, where the customer journey has moved almost entirely off-site into Reddit threads, Slack groups, and Discord conversations you'll never see.
Talia breaks down the difference between social image and self image in B2B buying, shares a case study where a QA software company won by speaking to the emotional reality that everyone in the company hates the QA team, and explains why your website has become a confirmation tool rather than a persuasion tool.
